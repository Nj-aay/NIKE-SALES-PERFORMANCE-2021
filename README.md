Project Overview

This project analyzes Nike sales performance across 2020–2021 using Microsoft Excel.

The goal was to turn raw sales data into a business-focused dashboard that shows how revenue and sales volume changed across time, retailers, states, product categories, and pricing.

The analysis focuses on understanding where sales were coming from and identifying patterns that could support decisions around inventory, pricing, retailer performance, and regional demand.

 Business Questions

The analysis was designed to answer:

What was Nike's total revenue and unit sales?
How did revenue change month by month?
Which retailers sold the most units?
Which states generated the highest sales volume?
Which product categories had the highest demand?
How did average prices differ between retailers?
How was revenue distributed across retailers?
Which state-retailer combinations generated notable revenue?
What areas would require further investigation?

 Data Cleaning & Preparation

Before analysis, the dataset was reviewed and prepared for reporting.

The cleaning process included:

Checking for duplicate transactions
Reviewing missing values
Standardizing date formats
Ensuring numerical fields were stored correctly
Reviewing categorical fields for inconsistent entries
Formatting financial fields consistently
Validating sales-related calculations
Preparing the data for aggregation and visualization

The cleaned data was then used to create the dashboard metrics and visualizations.

 Key Findings
Revenue

Total reported revenue was $1.58M, generated from 30,811 units.

Monthly performance

Revenue fluctuated significantly throughout the period, with approximately $250K in January compared with approximately $35K in June.

Retailer performance

Foot Locker recorded approximately 8,000 units, the highest visible retailer volume.

Geographic performance

California and Texas were the two largest states by visible unit volume, recording approximately 10,200 and 10,000 units, respectively.

Product demand

Men's Street Footwear recorded approximately 3,800 units, the highest visible product-category volume.

Pricing

The overall average price per unit was approximately $51.32, with differences observed across retailers.

 Business Implications

The analysis provides several areas for further business investigation:

Use retailer and state-level sales volume to support inventory allocation.
Monitor high-volume product categories for stock availability.
Compare revenue and units together when evaluating retailer performance.
Investigate significant monthly revenue fluctuations.
Examine retailer-level pricing alongside sales volume.
Explore the reasons behind differences in regional performance.

These findings describe patterns in the sales data; additional operational or customer data would be needed to establish the causes behind those patterns.
